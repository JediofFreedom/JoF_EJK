Movie Battles II lightning deflection effects

The lightning arc, flare, and impact effects and their blueLine,
lightningFlash, and sparkblue materials come from the Movie Battles II
Team's R22.3.01 release assets. Credit belongs to their original authors
and the Movie Battles II Team.

https://www.moviebattles.org/
https://community.moviebattles.org/pages/credits/
https://github.com/MBII/TextAssets/tree/ca3f32e41faf43851c19d146182d8f93f0de003a/MB_Effects2

The asset paths are namespaced for JoF so they affect only saber lightning
deflection. The cgame invokes them from the defender's replicated saber pose;
no Movie Battles II executable code or network state is included.
