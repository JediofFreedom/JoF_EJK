Movie Battles II lightning effects

The effects under effects/mp/lightning_reference, their three materials in
shaders/lightning_reference.shader, and textures in gfx/jof/lightning_reference
come from the Movie Battles II Team's R22.3.01 release assets. Credit for these
assets belongs to their original authors and the Movie Battles II Team.

https://www.moviebattles.org/
https://community.moviebattles.org/pages/credits/
https://github.com/MBII/TextAssets/tree/ca3f32e41faf43851c19d146182d8f93f0de003a/MB_Effects2

Original files:
  MB_Effects2.pk3: effects/Force/lightning.efx, lightningwide.efx,
                  lightning_arc.efx, lightning_impact.efx
                  shaders/effects.shader (blueLine and lightningFlash)
                  shaders/enhancedeffects.shader (sparkblue)
  MB_Effects.pk3:  gfx/misc/blueline.jpg, gfx/misc/sparkblue.jpg
  MBAssets2.pk3:   gfx/misc/lightningFlash.jpg

Only effect/material paths and text formatting were changed. Textures are
unmodified. Standard Jedi Academy impact shaders and sounds remain dependencies
of the original effects. These assets are used only by cg_lightningEnvironment 2.
